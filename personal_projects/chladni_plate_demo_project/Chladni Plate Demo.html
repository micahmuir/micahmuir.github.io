Chladni Plate Demo

As a one-off weekend project, I 3D printed a series of square and circular plates of PETG with space for a central embedded magnet. The plates were suspended with regular hair ties at each corner. Underneath the magnet, I ran a 50N electromagnet with a flyback diode and mosfet switches to modulate the magnetic field to produce controlled vibration. It was a lot of fun piping the hz modifier in from Touchdesigner to hunt down each plates harmonics.

As a proof of concept, I have intentions to use this as a module in a larger installation.
